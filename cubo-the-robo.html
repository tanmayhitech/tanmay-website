Cubo the Robo

A proactive intrusion detection system designed to alert owners at the exact moment unauthorized physical access occurs — before loss happens.

The Problem

Traditional surveillance systems are passive. Cameras record theft, but they do not prevent it. In small shops and local environments, unauthorized access to drawers and lockers often goes unnoticed until damage is already done.

The Solution

Cubo introduces awareness at the moment of access. The system detects physical intrusion in real time and immediately notifies the owner via Telegram alerts, enabling instant response.

Real-World Scenario

In a typical local shop, small amounts are often taken unnoticed. Cubo detects the drawer opening instantly, sends an alert, and allows the owner to verify the event immediately.

Core Technology

  • Arduino & ESP32 microcontroller logic
  • Ultrasonic distance sensing array
  • Real-time Telegram bot telemetry
  • Hardware-first security approach
