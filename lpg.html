IoT Safety System · 2026

Smart LPG
Safety System

An intelligent gas leak detection and automatic shut-off system designed to prevent domestic accidents using IoT automation, real-time monitoring, and Telegram-based emergency alerts.

The Problem

LPG leakage remains one of the leading causes of domestic fires, explosions, and accidental hazards. Traditional safety methods rely heavily on human awareness, which delays response during emergencies.

The Solution

This system continuously monitors gas concentration using the MQ-2 sensor. Once dangerous levels are detected, the system instantly activates warning alarms, shuts off the gas supply automatically using a servo mechanism, and sends emergency alerts through Telegram.

Real-World Scenario

A family leaves the kitchen unknowingly after a minor LPG leakage. Within seconds, the system detects abnormal gas levels, triggers the buzzer alarm, automatically rotates the servo to close the gas valve, and instantly notifies the owner on Telegram before the situation escalates.

Core Features

  • Real-time LPG gas detection
  • ESP32 Wi-Fi connectivity
  • Telegram emergency alerts
  • Automatic gas shut-off servo system
  • Red & green status indicators
  • Buzzer-based danger warning
