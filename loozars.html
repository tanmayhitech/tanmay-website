The Loozars

A culture-first streetwear brand built around expression, identity, and stories that Gen Z actually relates to.

Origin

Loozars was created to bridge the gap between fast mass-produced fashion and personal identity. The brand focuses on limited drops, meaningful graphics, and designs inspired by youth culture.

Brand Philosophy

Loozars does not chase generic seasonal trends. It documents youth emotions, raw subcultures, and expressive phases — turning them into wearable statements.

Founder’s Role

Tanmay Yadav leads product design, visual identity, creative direction, and digital presence of the brand. Loozars is co-founded with Mohd Zeeshan.

What Loozars Creates

  • Graphic streetwear apparel
  • Limited edition clothing drops
  • Youth-focused visual storytelling
  • Culture-driven merchandise

Brand Recognition & Media Features
