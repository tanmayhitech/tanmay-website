Background & Technical Journey

Tanmay Yadav (widely recognized across digital developer spaces as Tanmay Hitech) is an undergraduate developer pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) at Allenhouse Business School in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh.

His work bridges embedded electronics, microcontrollers (ESP32, Arduino), real-time API telemetry, and D2C product branding. Rather than developing isolated software models, Tanmay focuses on building tangible hardware security devices validated through real-world testing and competition showcases.

Key Projects & Systems

  • Cubo the Robo: Proactive intrusion detection system utilizing dual ultrasonic array distance sensing and ESP32 microcontrollers to deliver real-time encrypted Telegram alerts before loss occurs. [View Case Study]
  • Smart LPG Safety System: Automated domestic gas leak prevention system combining MQ-2 combustible gas sensors with high-torque servo valve shut-off mechanisms. [View Case Study]
  • Loozars Brand: Culture-first Gen Z apparel brand co-founded by Tanmay Yadav and Mohd Zeeshan focused on visual storytelling and limited streetwear capsule drops. [View Brand]
